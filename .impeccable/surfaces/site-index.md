# Surface brief: gallery (`site/index.html`)

Mode: Experience. The racquets lead from the first viewport; the interface recedes.

Audience and job: developers and designers picking racquet assets for tennis apps. They
compare frames, open one, check its specs, size, triangles and parts, and download it.

Constraints: two WebGL contexts at most; works without WebGL (list, specs, downloads);
375 px up; keyboard and reduced motion; no logos; styles read from the catalog; base path
`/stringbed/`. Build path: code-led (no image generation available).

## Direction contract

THESIS: The demo wall. Every frame hangs upright at one true scale in a continuous pro-shop
wall, sectioned by brand, so head shape, beam and colourway read by comparison. Refuses the
category default: a dark 3D-viewer grid of cards with a neon tennis-ball accent.

OWN-WORLD: A pale warm-grey wall (mid value, so black and white frames both read), ink type,
no accent colour of its own: the colourways are the palette. A shelf-edge label rail under
each row carries hang-tag labels in condensed Archivo; brand signs are wide condensed caps.
No cards, no shadows, no borders around racquets.

STORY: The visitor sees the whole lineup at once, narrows to a brand, takes one frame off the
wall, turns it in 3D, reads the spec tag and downloads the file.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A slim strip (name, one-line description, brand signs). Below it, at 1280 px,
all 14 racquets standing in two rows on rails. Primary action: each racquet is the link.

FORM: Retail demo wall, first of seven on the ordered list. seed: none (unavailable; the
launcher was not run under owner ruling R14).

Signature interaction: hover or focus turns a racquet on its axis to show the beam edge-on.
Motion grammar: one slow turn, exponential ease back; off under reduced motion.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
